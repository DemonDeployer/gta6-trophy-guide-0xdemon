Roadmap to the Platinum

Five phases, in order. Do phase one first — it holds the only missable trophy in the game.

  1. Phase 1 — Chapter 1, before you move on

    On the Lucia balcony meditation moment in Chapter 1, take the calm path: that is Positive Mental Attitude, the single missable trophy. Miss it and the only fix is a new playthrough or an earlier save.

  2. Phase 2 — Play the story

    Finish the campaign naturally. Most story trophies unlock as the plot advances, and the world opens up as you go.

  3. Phase 3 — Leonida side life

    Work through the activities: driving, flying, sports, minigames and stranger encounters. Use the checklist filters to sweep one kind at a time.

  4. Phase 4 — Collectibles sweep

    Clean up the collectible sets with the guide notes on each card. Check off each set as you finish it so nothing is counted twice.

  5. Phase 5 — Cleanup and Platinum

    Mop up whatever is left, then the Platinum — Leonida Legend itself — unlocks once the other 54 are earned.

About this guide

This guide covers all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. 28 trophies are hidden and 27 are visible. None require online play. Trophy names and descriptions are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them; every “How to unlock” note is written in our own words, and each card names the source it was checked against.

Sources checked: Exophase, Planet GTA 6, Push Square, PowerPyx, GTABase, PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements. A source is cited beside each trophy; no other guide’s text is reproduced here.

A note on the record: PlayStation publishes no public web trophy list for this game, so this guide cites the PSN-mirroring trackers listed above. The trackers disagree on one grade: Maximum Withdrawal is Silver according to the PSN-mirroring trackers, while one guide lists it as Bronze. This guide follows Silver and records the disagreement here.

Unconfirmed: four trophies have no confirmed unlock method at launch — Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, “Bad Luck, I Guess” and The Truth Is Out There — so they are marked UNCONFIRMED here rather than guessed at.
