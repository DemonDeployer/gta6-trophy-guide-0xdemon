Roadmap to the platinum

Six phases, in order. The story first, the 100% checklist last — and one warning that matters more than all the rest.

  1. Phase 1 — Play the story, save smart

    Finish the Prologue and Chapters 1–4, then the final mission. Make a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission: there is no mission replay. In Chapter 1, do Lucia’s balcony meditation at Jason’s house the morning after Down to Earth (see Positive Mental Attitude) — it is the one possibly missable trophy. Never turn on a cheat: cheats disable trophies.

  2. Phase 2 — Stranger strands

    Complete the mission chains for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus three more strangers, for Strangers & Freaks. Randy’s strand needs specific wish-list vehicles; Tyrone’s runs through the Kitty Litter Labs.

  3. Phase 3 — Activities

    250 stunts (397 exist), gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges, 20 different race wins, a Top Fun drone win at all 14 locations, Cal’s fishing challenge, one arcade game, and 50 of the 80 challenges.

  4. Phase 4 — Collectibles & the world

    3 card sets (15 for 100%), WAiNK scans at 100 locations, all 18 meditation spots, 100 vehicles discovered, and every corner of Leonida explored.

  5. Phase 5 — Crime & money

    5 bank vaults, an armored truck, a jewelry store undetected, one illegal operation per gang, $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent in shops, and all 8 garages ($160,000) with 10 vehicles registered to Scooter Bros.

  6. Phase 6 — 100% checklist & cleanup

    After the credits the checklist appears (Touch Pad > Progress > Stats > 100% Completion). It is lenient — about 60% true completion: 15 of 18 card sets, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts. Finish it for A Long Road, mop up what is left, and the platinum pops.

The one warning: Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy two guides flag as possibly missable — Lucia’s Chapter 1 balcony meditation vanishes after Chapter 1. Whether it is strictly required is unconfirmed, so do it anyway and keep that chapter-start save.

About this guide

Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation Network list as mirrored by Exophase, cross-checked against Planet GTA 6 (which cross-checks Exophase, PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements) and Push Square. PlayStation.com publishes no public web trophy list for the game, so the guide says where every fact comes from: each trophy below cites its sources. Guide text is written in this guide’s own words; it draws on the linked guides but never copies them.

One disagreement to be honest about: Push Square’s work-in-progress guide lists Maximum Withdrawal as Bronze, while Exophase, Planet GTA 6 and the other lists give it as Silver. This guide follows the majority and the tracker data (Silver), and flags the disagreement here rather than hiding it.

Four trophies (Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There) have no confirmed unlock method at launch — their entries say so plainly instead of guessing. Difficulty and time estimates come from PowerPyx (4/10, about 200 hours); Push Square estimates 300 hours or more at a leisurely pace.
